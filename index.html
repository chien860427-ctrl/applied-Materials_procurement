資訊傳播工程背景，擁有 6 年以上專案管理（PJM）與採購分析經驗。在團隊中始終扮演「Project Enabler」與跨部門主要窗口：主導軟硬體整合與 SaaS 專案，具備精準對齊內部研發、客戶與外部供應商的溝通實力。我能將過去專案管理經驗、API 串接規格與進度交付測試的系統化能力，直接應用於應材 Buyer 的工程圖面/規格對齊、跨部門需求整合及物料如期交付。

具備高度的數據分析力與商業損益意識。在過往 PLM 採購與供應鏈管理中，累積了深入分析歷史營運與供需數據、動態調整庫存商品、評估需求與利潤結構的基礎。在專案實務上，我擅長落實嚴格的時程推進、資源與成本預算控管，並能敏捷因應範疇變動與緊急需求變更；這份掌控力能無縫接軌應材採購核心工作—從精準執行 RFQ 詢比議價、PO 發行與合約管理，到靈活動態調度物料交期，在嚴控成本與範疇變更的同時，主動辨識潛在供應風險並實現成本優化。

在推進跨團隊協作時，擅長以流程導向思維運用 RCA 根本原因分析精準解決問題與交付異常；並曾導入 AI 工具建立標準化 SOP 與自動化工作流。我將運用這套結構化方法與敏捷力，協助應材推動採購流程簡化與建立高標準作業流程，為團隊嚴格把關採購成本與交期，確保專案與產線穩定推進。

I studied Information and Communication Engineering, and I have over 6 years of experience in project management and purchasing analysis. I usually act as the “Project Enabler” and the main point of contact, leading software, hardware, and SaaS projects while connecting R&D, clients, and suppliers.

The Buyer role at Applied Materials fits my background and key strengths very well:

  • Execution & Delivery — I turn complex requirements and API specs into clear delivery and test checklists, directly applicable to reading part specs and drawings, working with engineers, and ensuring on-time material delivery.
  • Cost & Scope Control — In PLM purchasing and supply chain work, I use operational data to adjust inventory, forecast demand, and protect margins. I manage tight timelines, budgets, and sudden scope changes — handling RFQs, issuing POs, adjusting delivery dates, and lowering costs while managing supply risks.
  • Problem Solving & SOPs — When urgent issues happen, I use Root Cause Analysis (RCA) to find and fix the real problem, and use AI tools to build clear SOPs and automate workflows for simpler daily operations.

I look forward to bringing my skills to Applied Materials to strictly manage costs and delivery times, keeping both projects and production lines running smoothly.
